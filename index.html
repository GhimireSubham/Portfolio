About

A Brief Overview.

I'm an aspiring front-end web developer and UI/UX design enthusiast, currently pursuing a B.Sc. (Hons) in Computing at Islington College, affiliated with London Metropolitan University. My studies have strengthened my foundation in both design thinking and modern web technologies.

My work sits at the intersection of design and development — I design interfaces for web applications, build responsive sites with modern front-end technologies, and apply UI/UX principles to make things people can actually use. I'm a lifelong learner, always exploring where good design and solid engineering meet.

  • Interested in Responsive Design, Wireframing & Prototyping
  • Currently exploring User-centred, e-commerce & accessible web experiences
