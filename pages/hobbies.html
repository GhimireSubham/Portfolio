Hobby 02

Gaming

My taste leans toward games with strong art direction and world-building. Here's what I play, what I've finished, and what I'm currently ignoring my assignments for.

Action RPG Elden Ring Brutal, beautiful, and completely disrespectful of my sleep schedule. Best open world in recent memory. Currently playing
Action Adventure Ghost of Tsushima The photo mode alone could be its own game. Every frame looks like a woodblock print. Completed
Narrative / RPG Cyberpunk 2077 Night City is one of the most dense and visually striking environments I've ever walked through. Completed
Open World Red Dead Redemption 2 The benchmark for world-building. I once spent 40 minutes just riding through a thunderstorm doing nothing. Completed
Action Adventure Spider-Man 2 No game makes traversal feel this good. Swinging through Manhattan at sunset is genuinely therapeutic. Completed
Shooter Valorant My friends dragged me in. Now I'm the one dragging others. It never ends. Ongoing
